Module Breakdown & Capability Overview

1. Projects & Kanban Pipeline

Create projects with customized categories. Assign Bill of Materials (BOM) parts directly, track target budgets with real-time cost meters, and drag-and-drop project cards across your pipeline from Planning to Completed.

2. Hardware Inventory & QR Bins

Track parts across physical storage bins (e.g., *Box 1 - Microcontrollers*). Generate printable QR code labels for physical bins and instantly flag low-stock items against minimum safety limits.

3. Manufacturing Methods

Personalize your workshop capabilities by toggling your active fabrication methods (FDM 3D printing, CNC milling, SLA resin, PCB etching, laser cutting) to match your lab setup.

4. Calculators & Serial Terminal

Use built-in Ohm's Law and LED drop resistor calculators on the fly. Connect microcontrollers over USB using the Web Serial API terminal to debug firmware directly in your browser.

💡 Pro Tip for Making the Most of MakerOS:

All your data is saved automatically in your browser's local storage. Use the Export and Import backup buttons in the sidebar footer anytime you switch computers or want to back up your engineering workspace.
